# GrandGridStudio — Brand Guide

**Extracted from actual product PDFs on 2026-04-04**

## Logo
- **File**: `Shop logo.jpg` in repo root
- **Design**: Silver/metallic crown icon constructed from interlocking grid lines (representing nonogram grids)
- **Text**: "GRAND GRID STUDIO" in silver serif capitals, letterspaced
- **Tagline**: "LOGIC FOR THE 1%" in smaller silver capitals beneath
- **Background**: Navy blue textured (leather/fabric texture)
- **Usage**: Etsy shop icon, watermarks, social media profile images, cover branding

## Visual Identity

### Colors
- **Primary Background**: Deep navy blue (~#1B1F3B / "Titan Noir")
- **Border/Accent**: Silver/light gray double-line border on all covers
- **Title Text**: Silver/light gray (serif font, large)
- **Series Name Text**: Muted gold/champagne (#C4A44A approximate)
- **Body Text**: Light gray/white
- **Puzzle Grids**: Black and white on white background
- **Printing Guides**: Same navy background with silver/gray text

### Typography
- **Brand Name** ("Grand Grid Studio"): Large serif font, silver/gray, title case
- **Series Title** (e.g., "THE TITANIC SERIES | VOLUME I"): Uppercase, sans-serif, muted gold
- **Puzzle Names** (e.g., "CAFE SERENADE"): Bold, gold/champagne, uppercase
- **Subtitles** ("From the Behemoth Series", "Grid Size: 250x250 | Level: Grandmaster"): Light gray, smaller, regular weight
- **Taglines**: Italic, light gray ("Hand-Crafted Logic for the 1% of Solvers", "Logic for the Elite 1%", "People, Portraits & Scenes of Daily Life")
- **Bottom tagline**: Gold/champagne, italic, uppercase ("THE ELITE 1% OF LOGIC SOLVERS", "FANTASY, MAGIC & MYSTERY", "FOR MASTER SOLVERS ONLY")

### Cover Layout (Consistent Across All Products)
1. Navy background with silver double-line border
2. "GRAND GRID STUDIO" centered at top (large, silver)
3. Series/Edition type below (large bold or "SINGLE MASTERPIECE EDITION")
4. Puzzle name in gold/champagne (centered, mid-page)
5. Series attribution + grid size + level (light gray, centered)
6. Bottom tagline (gold, italic)

### Two Cover Variants
- **Series Collections** (Titanic, Colossus, Behemoth): "Grand Grid Studio" → "THE [SERIES] SERIES | VOLUME I" → "15 [ADJECTIVE] NONOGRAMS" → subtitle → bottom tagline
- **Single Masterpiece Edition**: "GRAND GRID STUDIO" → "SINGLE MASTERPIECE EDITION" → puzzle name → "From the [Series] Series" → grid size + level → "THE ELITE 1% OF LOGIC SOLVERS"

## Product Structure

### Singles (4-page PDFs)
1. Cover page (navy, branded)
2. Puzzle page (white, grid with number clues, "Grand Grid Studio" watermark top-center)
3. Solution page (white, filled grid showing revealed artwork)
4. Back page (blank or minimal)

### Series Collections (multi-page PDFs)
1. Cover page
2. Printing Protocol page (navy background, practical instructions)
3. Puzzle pages (alternating: puzzle title page → puzzle grid → solution)
4. Solutions section at end

### Puzzle Page Layout
- Title top-left: puzzle name, "Puzzle" or "Solution", grid dimensions, "Recommended: [paper size]"
- "Grand Grid Studio" watermark centered above grid
- Grid: black lines on white, number clues along top and left edges
- Credit line: "Pixel Puzzle Artist" (likely the generation tool name)
- Black and white pixel art revealed in solutions

## Solved Artwork Subjects (Visible from Solutions)

- **Dragon's Wrath** (Titanic): A fierce dragon in dynamic pose, breathing fire/energy
- **Frozen Gaze Majesty** (Colossus): Close-up snow leopard face, intense eyes, detailed fur
- **Cafe Serenade** (Behemoth): Bustling cafe scene with multiple people at tables, atmospheric lighting

These are high-fidelity black-and-white pixel art — they look like detailed photographs or illustrations when the grid is filled in. This is the key selling point: what appears as a blank grid of numbers transforms into stunning artwork.

## Key Brand Messages
- "The Elite 1% of Logic Solvers"
- "Hand-Crafted Logic"
- "Titanic-Scale" puzzles
- Pure logic, no guessing, unique solutions
- Professional A2/A1/A0 printing optimized
- "Pixel Puzzle Artist" — the puzzles are art, not just puzzles

## What NOT to Do
- Never show solvable grids (more than 5x5 corner)
- Never use casual/beginner-oriented language — this brand targets elite solvers
- Never suggest these can be solved on regular paper — emphasize large-format printing
- Never promise speed — these are marathon challenges (10-25+ hours each)
