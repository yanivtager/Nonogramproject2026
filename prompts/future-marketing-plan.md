# Grand Grid Studio — Future Marketing Expansion Plan

**Prerequisite**: Phase 1 (Pinterest + Reddit automation) is running and showing results.
**Trigger to start Phase 2**: Consistent engagement on Pinterest/Reddit OR 10+ sales from organic traffic.

---

## Phase 2: Etsy Ads + Instagram (Month 2-3 after Phase 1 validates)

### Etsy Ads
- Start at **$1/day** (~$30/month)
- Target keywords: nonogram, picross, griddlers, logic puzzle printable, giant nonogram
- Monitor ROAS weekly — pause if no conversions after 2 weeks
- Your edge: almost no competition for 150x150+ nonograms on Etsy
- Market signal: 2-5K Etsy searches/month for "nonogram", 10M+ app downloads prove the audience exists

### Instagram (@GrandGridStudio)
- **Content**: Repurpose Pinterest images, no new creation needed
- **Posts**: 2x/week — solved puzzle art, grid comparisons, puzzle facts
- **Reels**: 1x/week — zoom-in solution reveals, "can you guess what this is?"
- **Stories**: Polls ("which grid size scares you?"), behind-the-scenes
- **Hashtags**: #nonogram #picross #logicpuzzle #puzzleart #pixelart #brainteaser #printablepuzzle
- **Effort**: ~2 hrs/week
- **Cost**: $0 (organic only)

---

## Phase 3: TikTok + Facebook Groups (Month 4-5)

### TikTok (@GrandGridStudio)
- **Format 1**: Timelapse solve videos — satisfying, high share potential
- **Format 2**: Grid reveal shorts — black cells filling in to reveal image, ASMR-like
- **Format 3**: "Can you solve this corner?" — interactive challenge
- **Format 4**: Size comparison — "This is a 25x25... now THIS is a 250x250" — shock value
- **Post frequency**: 2-3x/week
- **Effort**: 3 hrs/week (batch-produce)
- **Cost**: $0
- **Risk**: Audience skews young/casual, conversion to PDF buyers is uncertain. Test and measure.

### Facebook Groups (passive participation)
- Join 5-10 puzzle/nonogram/brain teaser groups
- Share solving tips and solution art, engage genuinely
- Soft-promote only when relevant (not every post)
- Do NOT create a Facebook page (organic reach is dead)
- **Effort**: 30 min/week
- **Cost**: $0

---

## Phase 4: Scale Winners (Month 6+)

Based on 6 months of data, double down on what converts:

- Pinterest drives sales → increase frequency, add Idea Pins
- Reddit converts → create r/GrandGridStudio community
- Etsy Ads profitable → increase to $3-5/day
- Instagram grows → add paid promotions ($20-50/month)
- TikTok gets views → batch-produce more video content
- Nothing works → pivot to email list + SEO blog

---

## Phase 5: Advanced (2027)

- **Email newsletter**: Free mini-puzzle PDF as lead magnet, collect emails, announce new products
- **YouTube**: Long-form solve videos, "how nonograms work" educational content
- **Collaborations**: Reach out to puzzle YouTubers/streamers for reviews
- **Discord community**: Solver leaderboards, timed challenges, exclusive preview puzzles
- **Vol. 2 launch**: Built-in audience across all channels
